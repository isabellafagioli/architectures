Order & inventory flow — two options
Today there is no real-time stock check between VTEX and the vendor network, and no official system-of-record inventory. Two ways to handle that going forward:

Option 2 — Integration (middleware · HFA)
Sequence matters here: the integration app pulls Catalog, Price and Inventory from HFA and pushes them into VTEX asynchronously, continuously, before any order happens. By the time a customer checks out, that data is already in VTEX — no live call needed at checkout.
